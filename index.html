Well, well, well traveler... you have just stumbled into my humble abode; one of dangerous tread and divine conqueresness. The mighty jungle of fisfortune, and some might even say... death.

Only thee who ought to explore these forgotten fatherlands will have smouldered their braveness into the everglades of the strutting flora and fauna.
Beware my friend, as your fragile mortal coil sinks deep within the terra, and your stay shall nevermore ensue long.
